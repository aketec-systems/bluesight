Two ways to meet

The phone finds people in the same room for free. The internet is optional, and when you do use it, everything goes in one small request.

Nearby, over Bluetooth

At a party, church, campus, market or club, phones find each other within about 30 m. Cards, likes, matches and chat all go over Bluetooth, with no data used.

Around, here or back home

Browse people around town, or pick any city from the built-in list. A deck of 20 people is about 4 KB.

Chat from anywhere

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. When your match is in range they go over Bluetooth; otherwise they ride the next sync, about 0.1 KB each.

Search back home

Most of us have two places: where we live now and where home is. Pick a city and start matching before you land. People there see you as Not in town, never a distance, so no one is misled about how close you are. When you arrive, the same match carries on over Bluetooth for free.
